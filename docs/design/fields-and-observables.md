# Fields and Observables

## Core Concepts

### Fields
- Fundamental spaces that define possible properties
- Establish relationships between properties
- Define measurement rules and constraints
- Determine valid state spaces

### Observables
- First-class representations of properties that may be observed
- Have identity and uniqueness within their field
- Represent properties in various states until measured
- Subject to field-defined relationships
